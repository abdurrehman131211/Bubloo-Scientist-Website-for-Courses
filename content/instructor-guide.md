# Bubloo Scientist Academy — Instructor Guide

## Course positioning
AI Foundations is the first Bubloo Scientist Academy product. It is designed for beginners who want a practical introduction without a technical prerequisite.

## Recommended delivery
- 7 lessons
- 5–8 minute audio per lesson
- 10–15 minutes total learner time per lesson including activity
- One quiz per lesson
- One downloadable workbook
- Completion certificate can be added later

## Production workflow
1. Record the audio script.
2. Export one MP3 per lesson.
3. Add each MP3 to the lesson page.
4. Keep transcripts available for accessibility.
5. Replace the demo enrollment CTA with a real checkout provider.
6. Add a private learner area only when there are paying students.

## Brand direction
Use the Bubloo Scientist visual identity: futuristic technology, premium dark backgrounds, cyan/blue highlights, clean typography, subtle glow, and strong educational hierarchy.

## Suggested launch price
Founding launch: PKR 1,499.

Suggested later standard price: PKR 2,499 after collecting learner feedback and improving the course.

Do not promise employment, income, grades, or guaranteed results. Sell the learning experience and concrete course contents.
