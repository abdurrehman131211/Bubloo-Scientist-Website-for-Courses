# Quiz Bank — AI Foundations

1. What is AI? **Answer:** A broad field of computer systems performing tasks associated with intelligence.
2. What is generative AI? **Answer:** AI designed to generate content such as text, images, audio, or code.
3. What improves prompt usefulness? **Answer:** Clear goals, context, constraints, and desired format.
4. Should important AI-generated claims be verified? **Answer:** Yes.
5. What can AI help with during research? **Answer:** Brainstorming, organization, summarization of supplied material, and question generation.
6. What should a researcher do with important claims? **Answer:** Check reliable sources.
7. What is a useful role for AI in creativity? **Answer:** Brainstorming, drafting, iteration, and variations.
8. Who makes the final creative decisions? **Answer:** The creator/user.
9. What can AI do for programmers? **Answer:** Explain code, suggest implementations, generate tests, and help debug.
10. What should beginners do with AI-generated code? **Answer:** Understand, run, test, and modify it.
11. What should not be casually pasted into AI tools? **Answer:** Passwords, private keys, and confidential information.
12. What is a workflow? **Answer:** A repeatable process for completing a task.
13. Give an example workflow. **Answer:** Capture → Clarify → Generate → Verify → Edit → Save.
14. Should AI be used for every task? **Answer:** No; use it where it genuinely reduces effort or improves learning.
15. Why save successful prompts? **Answer:** To create reusable templates and reduce repeated work.
16. Can AI be wrong while sounding confident? **Answer:** Yes.
17. What three safety ideas anchor the course? **Answer:** Protect private information, verify important claims, and keep human judgment involved.
18. Why check dates? **Answer:** Information can become outdated.
19. What should a first AI project be like? **Answer:** Small enough to finish and useful for learning.
20. What is the user's role when using AI? **Answer:** Direct, evaluate, edit, verify, and decide.
21. What is the course's overall mindset? **Answer:** AI is a tool that can extend your abilities when used thoughtfully.
